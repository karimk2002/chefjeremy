# essencexp.com: project context

Everything known about the Essence & Experience website as of 13 November 2026, for a new Claude project picking up the work. Sources are listed in §15. Where sources disagree, both versions are given and the point is marked **to confirm**.

---

## 1. In one paragraph

Essence & Experience (E&E) is a Singapore-based hospitality advisory founded by Michelin-starred chef **Jérémy Gillon**, with **Agilson Valle** as managing partner. It works with hotels, restaurant groups, developers, investors, operators and brands on concepts, F&B strategy, pre-opening, market entry, operations, talent, and premium events and collaborations. **essencexp.com** is its company website: **unequivocally B2B**, darker and more corporate than Jérémy's personal site **jeremygillon.com**. The two sites are built as **one project** by Karim Kouchrad (web designer and developer, working remotely, not in Singapore). Today essencexp.com redirects to Jérémy's Wix site; both sites move off Wix to custom code on hosting the client owns.

## 3. How E&E relates to jeremygillon.com

- **Two separate websites that link to each other, built as one project.** Clients move between private dining, events and consulting, so each site sends visitors to the other.
- **jeremygillon.com** is Jérémy's personal site: biography, Michelin career, **private dining** (B2C), collaborations, press. Light: cream and forest (Art Direction v2).
- **essencexp.com** is the company: consulting, events and collaborations (B2B). Darker palette, same font and frame, a related mark.
- **Private dining belongs to jeremygillon.com only.** E&E's navigation has no private dining page; it has a link in the menu and footer pointing to jeremygillon.com. On jeremygillon.com, a link reading "Founder & Culinary Director, Essence & Experience" points to essencexp.com.
  - Watch out: Agilson's own E&E-branded sales deck (§10) sells "Private Dining | Events | Consultancy" as E&E services. On the website, keep private dining on Jérémy's site and treat event formats (cocktail, canapé, seated, standing) as E&E events.
- **Build order:** the jeremygillon.com Private Dining page is built first (decided 26 Oct); then jeremygillon.com; the E&E style sheet in week 2; essencexp.com in weeks 3 to 5.

### Included (both sites)

Responsive and accessible; SEO foundations (keywords, titles and descriptions, image alt text, sitemap, Google Search Console; for E&E: hospitality consulting); enquiry forms delivering to the client's inbox with a signed automatic reply; privacy-friendly visitor statistics; move off Wix (domains transferred, existing email kept working, old addresses redirected); two rounds of changes on each site's preview; 30 days of fixes after each launch; a short handover guide. Code, domains and content belong to the client once paid in full.

**E&E art direction:** derived from Art Direction v2, so no second audit. It is delivered as **one style sheet with one round of changes**, and the build starts after it's approved.

### Not included

Writing articles or SEO content, Google Ads, social media, photography, translations, extra pages, third-party costs. **Next phase** (separate proposal after launch): Google Ads (Singapore, Thailand, Hong Kong), organic social, journal articles, care plan. A **concierge page** for SEO and future ads was proposed on 26 Oct (§11).

### Commercial (client-facing figures only)

€1,700 for both sites (€2,000 less €300 thanks for an introduction). €650 paid on 24 Oct (brand audit €200 + deposit €450). **€525 is due when essencexp.com goes live**, and €525 when jeremygillon.com does. About six weeks from the week of 26 Oct; launch around the week of 30 Nov, provided the materials arrive.

### Stack and hosting

Custom code. Jérémy's site was first built in **Astro 5** (static, plus one API route for the enquiry email); the plan is to move both sites to **Cloudflare Pages** (the enquiry route becomes a Pages Function; Vercel Hobby is non-commercial). Domains move from Wix (registrar and email setup still unknown). Wix access is only needed at deployment.

## 5. Audience and positioning

- **B2B first:** hotel and resort owners and operators, restaurant groups, developers, investors (Agilson's capital-markets background), brands (luxury, wine and spirits), event companies and agencies, tourism bodies.
- **Concierges** (luxury event and travel middlemen) mainly request **VIP private dining**. Those enquiries belong to Jérémy's private dining offer; a concierge landing page is planned for later (§11).
- **Discretion:** private clients are often wealthy families. Show no client details unless permitted; describe confidential work by type ("pre-opening of a 60-seat restaurant, Bangkok").
- **Proposition, from E&E's own documents:** Michelin-level culinary direction combined with hospitality strategy and commercial discipline. Agilson's line: "a cross-cultural and commercially focused perspective at the intersection of hospitality, real estate, investment and lifestyle." Markets: Singapore and Asia-Pacific, with experience across Europe and Latin America.

## 10. Art direction

**Inherited from Art Direction v2** (Jérémy's approved system): the same fonts and frame. **Fraunces** (weight 300, optical size around 36) for headlines and serif text, **Inter** 400/500 for everything else, both free and self-hosted. Jérémy's palette: cream #FBF7EE ground, ivory #F1ECE0, forest #20482D accent, moss #557056, charcoal #23282B body, stone #6A675E captions.

**E&E is the darker sibling:** luxury black, charcoal and muted gold (from Agilson's blueprint). The forms preview used: ground #151716, surface #1E211F, text #ECE6DA, muted #A19B8E, gold #BC9762, lines #343832. These are a first take, to settle in the E&E style sheet.

- A brass #B98746 and dark green #063D31 palette appears in an earlier business-plan deck Agilson saw elsewhere. It is **not** the official direction.

**Logo:** **no Essence & Experience logo file exists.** A related mark is part of the E&E style sheet work, in the same family as Jérémy's monogram. Jérémy's monogram has two vector options, A ("the edit") and B ("the redesign"), both forest on transparent, fork removed; his choice is pending. A small "ESSENCE & EXPERIENCE" wordmark image appears in the PR card, but there's no usable file.

**Images:**
- Photos come before logos: photos are the priority for building now.
- **Never use the AI-generated images** from the business-plan deck (a chef who is not Jérémy).
- Several images in the PR card look like stock (mountains, an empty dining room, an infinity pool). Use only photos the client owns or has rights to.
- On Jérémy's side, no guests appear in any photo.

**Copy:**
- English.
- Words such as "curated" and "elevated", banned on jeremygillon.com, are tolerated in E&E copy, but plain concrete language is still preferred.
- No prices.
