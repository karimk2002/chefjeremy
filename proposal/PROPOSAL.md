# Jérémy Gillon × Essence & Experience
## Website proposal: one project, two avenues

Prepared for Jérémy Gillon and Agilson Valle
By Karim Kouchrad · 15 October 2026 · valid until 15 November 2026

---

## 1. What we agreed

On our call of 15 October we agreed to build **two separate websites that link to each other**, as **one project**:

- **jeremygillon.com**: Jérémy's own site. Who he is, his cuisine, his Michelin career, and private dining.
- **essencexp.com**: the Essence & Experience company site, for hotels, restaurant groups, brands and partners.

Clients move between private dining, events and consulting, so each site sends visitors to the other. Both sites share one family look: the same font and frame, similar marks, sage/green/beige. Jérémy's site is lighter and E&E's is darker. Both sites leave Wix and are custom-coded on hosting that you own.

## 2. The two avenues

### Avenue 1: jeremygillon.com (personal, light)

Built on **Art Direction v2**, which you approved: cream, forest, herbs and the monogram.

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| Home | "Michelin-starred chef, Singapore & beyond" |
| Jérémy | Biography, culinary identity (Savoie, herbs and vegetables), career and Michelin milestones |
| Private Dining | The eight-screen evening already designed, "The Alps, at your table", plus **2 to 3 past menus or experiences**, with no client names and no guests in frame |
| Collaborations & Guest Chef | Residencies, brand dinners, special projects |
| Press | Articles, features, recognition |
| Contact | Two enquiry types: private dinner, or collaboration |

There is also a privacy notice. A discreet line reading *"Founder & Culinary Director, Essence & Experience"* links to essencexp.com.

> Your suggested navigation had nine items. I recommend joining *Jérémy*, *Culinary Identity* and *Career & Michelin* into one page so the story reads as a whole. If you prefer three separate pages, the content stays the same.

### Avenue 2: essencexp.com (company, darker)

**Art direction:** this is derived from v2, not a new audit. It uses the same font and frame, a related mark, and a darker palette (charcoal, black, muted gold) for consulting. You receive it as one style sheet with one round of changes, and the build starts after you approve it.

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| Home | Company proposition, services at a glance, proof, the founders |
| Services | Advisory, concepts, F&B strategy, pre-opening, market entry, operations, talent and leadership, brand collaborations, events. One page, with a section and a direct link for each service |
| Collaborations & Events | Guest-chef dinners, residencies, brand and corporate events |
| Selected Work | Project types and proof. A name or logo appears only where its owner has given permission |
| Team | Jérémy (Founder & Culinary Director, with "Discover Jérémy" linking to jeremygillon.com) and Agilson (Managing Partner), each with his own bio page |
| Journal | Article layout for SEO, LinkedIn and Substack. The template is included. Writing the articles is a separate service (see §7) |
| Contact | Business enquiry form: project type, location, stage, timeline, budget range |

There is also a privacy notice. Private dining appears in the footer and links to jeremygillon.com.

### Included on both sites

- Works on phone, tablet and desktop. Fast and accessible.
- **SEO foundations:** target keywords (private dining, private chef at home, chef events in Singapore; hospitality consulting for E&E), page titles and descriptions, image metadata and alt text, sitemap, Google Search Console.
- Enquiry forms that deliver to your inbox, with a signed automatic reply.
- Privacy-friendly visitor statistics.
- **Move off Wix:** domains connected, existing email (e.g. info@jeremygillon.com) kept working, old addresses redirected.
- **Two rounds of changes** on each site's preview before launch.
- A short handover guide.
