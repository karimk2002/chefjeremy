## 7. Hosting and moving off Wix

Both sites are **custom-coded** with no page builder, so you have full control over design, speed and SEO, and you are free to choose where they live. I recommend Cloudflare; three alternatives are listed for comparison:

| Option | Hosting | Domains | Approx. yearly cost, both sites | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Cloudflare** (recommended) | Free, commercial use allowed | Registered at cost, about US$11 per .com a year | **about US$22** (domains only) | Fast in Singapore, Thailand and Hong Kong. Hosting, domains and DNS in one account |
| B. Netlify | Free plan, paid from US$9/month if traffic grows | Stay at any registrar | about US$22–130 | Good alternative to Cloudflare |
| C. Vercel | Pro plan, about US$20/month (the free plan is for non-commercial use only) | Stay at any registrar | about US$260 | Only worth it for more advanced features later |
| D. IONOS | Classic shared hosting | Domains at IONOS, no transfer fee | Depends on the plan; .com renewal is about US$20 | Familiar, but slower and less flexible than A or B |

Whichever option you choose, the account is **in your name** and I am added as a collaborator.

### How the domains leave Wix

Both domains (jeremygillon.com and essencexp.com) are currently with Wix. Moving them is routine:

1. **Before anything moves,** I record every DNS setting, especially the email records, so that info@jeremygillon.com keeps working.
2. The new sites go live on a **preview address** first, so nothing changes for visitors until they are approved.
3. In the Wix account: **Domains → Domain actions → Transfer away from Wix.** Wix unlocks the domain and emails a transfer code to the domain's owner email address.
4. I start the transfer at the new registrar with that code. The transfer includes one extra year of registration, added on top of the current expiry date, so nothing is lost.
5. The transfer takes **up to 5–7 days**. The email records are recreated straight away, so mail is never interrupted.
6. Once both sites are live on their domains, you can **cancel the Wix plan**.

**Two points to check now:**
- A domain cannot be transferred within **60 days** of its registration, of a previous transfer, or of a change to the owner's details. Please don't change the owner's name or email in Wix before the transfer.
- If your email (Google Workspace) was **bought through Wix**, the email subscription stays with Wix after the domain moves. It keeps working once its records are recreated at the new registrar, and we can move the subscription directly to Google later if you prefer.

If a domain was only connected to Wix but is registered elsewhere, no transfer is needed: I simply point it to the new sites.
