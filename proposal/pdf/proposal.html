How the domains leave Wix

  1. Every current setting is recorded first, especially email, so info@jeremygillon.com keeps working.
  2. The new sites go live on a preview address. Nothing changes for visitors until you approve them.
  3. In Wix: Domains → Domain actions → Transfer away from Wix. Wix unlocks the domain and emails a transfer code to its owner.
  4. The transfer starts at the new provider with that code. It includes one extra year of registration, added to the current expiry date.
  5. It takes up to 5–7 days. Email settings are recreated straight away, so mail is never interrupted.
  6. Once both sites are live on their domains, the Wix plan can be cancelled.
Two things to know now. A domain cannot move within 60 days of a change to its owner’s name or email, so please leave those untouched in Wix. If your email was bought through Wix, that subscription stays with Wix after the move and keeps working; it can move to Google later if you prefer.
